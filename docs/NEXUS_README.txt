Eleanor Story Expand 1.14 — Nexus package
==========================================

中文
----
她从小被教导要俯视下层区。现在，她必须在那里活下去。

“呵，你们下层区管这种地方叫‘商店’啊？”

她是上层区最大水培农场前负责人的独女。母亲被以挪用公款罪关押，家族一夜崩塌，她跌进自己曾最看不起的下层区。
你，是这里的一家当铺老板。

这不是用钱刷好感的恋爱 MOD。她不会因为几次施舍就喜欢你。你要靠识货、开价、留证和承担风险，先成为她愿意信任的人。

她的骄傲不会凭空消失
她仍然挑剔、嘴硬、看不起下层区；也会在真正被帮助时别扭地道谢。你不会一开始就被她仰慕，而是先被她记住，再被她认可。

关系由实际经营行为推进
公平收购、明显让利、趁火打劫，她都会记住；一件来路可疑的货物、一次该不该提醒她的判断，都会影响你们之间的信任。关键结果来自你真正做了什么，而不是“好感 +1”的选项。

阶级反差，但不是救世主与花瓶
她会主动回礼、当面维护你，也会坚持自己的判断。她要的是平等，不是一个替她做决定的人。你也不是隐藏大佬，只是靠眼光、账本和人脉吃饭的店主。

保留原作人物与世界观
假叔叔、黑市打手、治安部、帕斯泰尔、营养果、水培农场都沿用原作设定；原作 Eleanor 的尖锐、自尊和行动力会保留下来，再往前推进。

两种开局
普通模式从第 23 天开始，保留原版节奏。
可选提前开场从第 12 天开始，不送好感、声望或金钱，仍走完整成长线。

内容体量
23 章核心剧情，含多条分支、后日谈与结局页。
6 万字以上剧情文本；正常单路线体验约经营到第 70 天。
2 位新角色，13 套服装形象，每套约 10 个表情差分。
简体中文 / English。
复用原版交易、检视、举报、拘捕、新闻和经营流程，不新建后间或独立案卷 UI。
旧存档默认受保护，可选择重置。

画面与兼容
本 Nexus 包已内置经作者授权的《少女当铺 / CutePortraits》埃莉诺基础立绘，以及本 MOD 的莉迪亚、母亲、女仆和礼服立绘；无需另装《少女当铺》。
请不要同时启用《少女当铺 / CutePortraits》或其他替换同一立绘的 MOD，避免重复贴图或互相覆盖。
如果其他 MOD 覆盖了当前可见立绘，本 MOD 会让位，并暂停表情切换，避免闪烁。
1.11 起，MOD 设置新增「立绘 / Portraits → Eleanor portrait mode」，可选 Auto / Force / Other 三种立绘模式。
1.14 起，游戏版本号不再作为停用剧情的前置条件；游戏补丁更新后继续加载已有存档。

为什么值得一玩
如果你在玩原版时对 Eleanor 有过兴趣，却觉得她的个人线太短、太晚，这个 MOD 会给她一条更完整的个人线。

如果你喜欢经营 + 人物关系，喜欢用报价、眼光和风险判断赢得信任，而不是靠选项刷数值；如果你希望女主角有自己的判断和选择权，而不只是奖励，这个 MOD 就是为这种体验做的。

你的选择会改变故事的走向。至于最后会走到哪里，进店之后，你自己判断。

四条主要路线与结局
恋爱线：你和埃莉诺一起在下层相恋、相伴，一起坚强地走下去。
后宫线：你将会同时攻略埃莉诺、以及本 MOD 中加入的一位新角色。
侍从线：你成为埃莉诺的主人，并且帮助她重回上层区。无论她多么耀眼，你是她唯一的王。
革命军线：你将和加入革命军的埃莉诺以战友身份一同经历生死的考验。
另有朋友结局：不发展恋爱关系，但保留彼此的信任与来往。

实用信息
版本 1.14；兼容游戏 1.0.0.15 / 1.0.0.14 / 1.0.0.13 / 0.46D（build 26525552 或更新）。游戏版本号不再作为停用剧情的前置条件。
解压 EleanorRomance 文件夹到 Probably Stolen/Mods/EleanorRomance，在模组菜单启用并重启游戏。
不需要其他 MOD。手动版与 Steam 工坊版只保留一个，不要同时使用；也不要同时安装两个 Nexus 副本。

旧存档
从未订阅过本 MOD 的存档，在首次开店时会触发“旧档案”事件；无论原埃莉诺进度如何，可选择重置埃莉诺的故事，并从本 MOD 剧情的第 1 章重开。
完成任一现行结局后，下一次开店空闲时“下层区档案员”会携带归档卷宗来访，可选择把本轮剧情翻回共同线、尝试另一条路线；已获得物品、真结局元进度与已经发生的世界效果保留。

English
-------
She was raised to look down on the Lower Level. Now she has to survive it.

“So this is what you Lower Level people call a ‘shop’?”

She is the only daughter of the former director of the Upper Level's largest hydroponic farm. Her mother is arrested for embezzlement, her family collapses overnight, and she falls into the Lower Level she once despised.
You own a pawnshop.

This is not a romance you can buy with gifts. She will not fall for a few handouts. You earn her trust by knowing value, making fair offers, keeping records and taking risks.

Her pride does not disappear
She is still picky, sharp-tongued and convinced the Lower Level is beneath her. When you genuinely help her, her thanks come out awkward and reluctant. You do not start as someone she admires. You start as someone she remembers — and then, slowly, someone she respects.

The relationship grows from what you actually do
A fair purchase, a generous price, an opportunistic squeeze — she notices all of it. A suspicious crate, a warning you could give or withhold, a risk you take on her behalf: these matter more than a “+1 affection” button. Key outcomes come from real decisions, not from picking the flattering line.

Class contrast, not savior and trophy
She gives back. She defends you to your face and behind your back. She insists on her own judgment. What she wants is equality, not someone who decides for her. You are not a hidden billionaire or a chosen one — just a shopkeeper who lives by a good eye, a ledger and the people he knows.

The original world stays intact
The fake uncle, the black-market enforcers, Security, Pastel Inc., Nutrifruit and the hydroponic farms remain part of the story. Eleanor keeps her sharpness, pride and drive; the mod gives her more room to use them.

Two ways to begin
Normal start: the remade story begins on day 23, preserving the original pacing.
Early start: an optional 0-point perk starts the story on day 12 without giving free affection, reputation or money. The same growth line, just earlier.

What you get
A 23-chapter core story with multiple branches, epilogues and ending pages.
More than 60,000 Chinese characters of story text; a normal single-route playthrough runs to around in-game day 70.
Two new characters, 13 outfits in total, with about 10 expression variants per outfit.
Simplified Chinese and English.
Native trade, inspection, reporting, arrest, news and shop systems; no separate backroom or case-file UI.
Old saves are protected by default; an optional reset is available.

For the best look
This Nexus package already bundles the authorized CutePortraits / Maiden's Pawnshop Eleanor base portraits, along with this mod's Lydia, Mother, maid and gala portraits. The separate CutePortraits / Maiden's Pawnshop mod is not required.
Do not enable CutePortraits / Maiden's Pawnshop or another mod that replaces the same portraits alongside this package; use one portrait source to avoid duplicate or overriding textures.
If another mod overrides the visible portrait, this mod yields and pauses expression switching instead of forcing its own art back.
Since 1.11, the mod settings include “Portraits → Eleanor portrait mode” with Auto, Force and Other options.
Since 1.14, game version strings no longer gate the story; game patch updates keep loading existing saves.

Why it is worth playing
If you were interested in Eleanor in the base game but found her personal line too short or too late, this mod gives her the full story she deserved.

If you like shopkeeping and character relationships, and prefer earning trust through prices, judgment and risk instead of farming dialogue options; if you want a heroine with her own mind and the right to choose, not a reward — this is for you.

Your choices change where the story goes. Where it ends is something you decide after you open the shop.

The four main routes and endings
Romance route: you and Eleanor fall in love, stand together in the Lower Level, and keep going.
Harem route: pursue both Eleanor and a new character added by this mod.
Attendant route: you become Eleanor's master and help her return to the Upper Level. However brightly she shines, you are her only king.
Revolution route: join the revolution with Eleanor as comrades-in-arms and face life-and-death trials together.
There is also a friends ending: no romance, but the trust and visits remain.

Practical notes
Version 1.14; compatible with Probably Stolen 1.0.0.15 / 1.0.0.14 / 1.0.0.13 / 0.46D (build 26525552 or later). Game version strings no longer gate the story.
Extract the EleanorRomance folder into Mods\EleanorRomance, enable it in the Mods menu and restart.
No other mods are required. Use either the manual copy or the Steam Workshop copy — not both. Do not install two Nexus copies at the same time.

Existing saves
A save that has never subscribed to this mod will trigger an “old file” event on its first shop day. Regardless of prior Eleanor progress, you can choose to reset her story and restart from chapter 1 of the mod's storyline.
After completing any current ending, the Archivist visits on a later free shop day and offers to reset the route so you can try another line. Received items, true-ending meta progress and completed world effects stay.
