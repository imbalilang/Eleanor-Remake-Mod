1.01 | Harem ornaments affect all Upper Level customers

• The three harem showcase ornaments previously recognized only three customer IDs (upperLowerVisitor, upperLowerVisitorLuxury, ulDiscerningShopper); the water stockpiler and other proper Upper Level customers were not counted, so the patron budget and gratitude tip did not apply.
• Eligibility now uses FACTION_UPPER_LEVEL and covers all Upper Level buyers, including SELLNBUY customers: water stockpiler, private chef, hospital supplier, upper maid, private security contractor and others.
• Story clients, native Eleanor's scripted appearances, other mods and non-buyers remain excluded; black market, Lower Level and scavengers are unaffected.
• The ornaments still require real showcase display, with the tip capped at 150 per visit. Only the eligibility range changed; rewards, routes and endings are unchanged; 1.00's portrait fix remains.
