[h2]上层区的富家千金爱上在下层区开当铺的我：埃莉诺剧情扩展[/h2]

“呵，你们下层区管这种地方叫‘商店’啊？”

她是上层区最大水培农场前负责人的独女。母亲被以挪用公款罪关押，家族一夜崩塌，她跌进自己曾最看不起的下层区。

你，是这里的一家当铺老板。

靠识货、开价、留证和承担风险，先成为她愿意信任的人。你们可以成为平等的恋人、继续做朋友，也可以选择一份双方说清条件的侍从约定。她的骄傲、判断与救母决心始终保留。

[h2]English[/h2]
Version 0.72 shortens Wilde preparation descriptions and introduces the first housing project through dialogue. Returning-visit openings and standalone feedback now give clearer context while keeping the original tone. Waiting dialogue names the current project. Story schedules, route conditions, payment and reward rules remain unchanged.

The optional attendant romance sits alongside the original equal romance, friendship and mother/research routes. Agree on clear terms, prepare four projects through Wilde's native upgrade interface, and help Eleanor establish a business under her own name. Preparations can wait and do not cancel an existing romance or rescue result. The protected-research route gains an independent case review for all relationship paths.

Includes original-style and Maiden's Pawnshop-style maid portraits, full Chinese/English text (2073 keys each), and precise continuation for supported legacy saves stalled after the old 800-credit choice, without retroactive charges. Normal start on day 23; optional early-start perk on day 12. All-ages. Back up saves before updating; downgrading is not guaranteed.
