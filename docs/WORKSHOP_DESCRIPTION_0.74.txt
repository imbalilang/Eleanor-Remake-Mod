[h2]上层区的富家千金爱上在下层区开当铺的我：埃莉诺剧情扩展[/h2]

“呵，你们下层区管这种地方叫‘商店’啊？”

她是上层区最大水培农场前负责人的独女。母亲被以挪用公款罪关押，家族一夜崩塌，她跌进自己曾最看不起的下层区。
你，是这里的一家当铺老板。

靠识货、开价、留证和承担风险，先成为她愿意信任的人。你们可以成为平等的恋人、继续做朋友，也可以选择一份双方说清条件的侍从约定。她的骄傲、判断与救母决心始终保留。

[h2]English[/h2]
An expanded Eleanor story with equal romance, friendship and an optional attendant relationship, four story-gated Wilde projects, five Upper Level return visits, real shop interactions and an independent case review. Original-style and Maiden's Pawnshop-style maid portraits included; no other mod is required.

Version 0.74 adds daily business dividends. After the first order succeeds, 200 credits accrue per game day; after the business opens, the rate rises to 600. Eleanor settles the accumulated balance during her own visits. Delayed visits never erase earnings. Wilde's projects show rewards and balances. Existing completed saves begin accruing after their first updated load, without invented historical income.

The attendant relationship now develops through cooperation, demonstrated judgment during the rescue and growing reliance afterwards. The first private address and maid outfit are delayed to chapter 19, with the formal curtsy after romance confirmation. The earlier box incident is addressed sooner; established legacy relationships keep their history. Includes a subtle vanilla maid eye retouch and matching English text, with 2109 keys per language. All 41 offline regression groups and the native mod scan pass.

Normal start on day 23; optional early-start perk on day 12. All-ages. Back up saves before updating; downgrading is not guaranteed.
