1.12：后宫线手镯持有判定修复

- 后宫线手镯资格改为按买入／卖出／回购／赠送事件计数，不再依赖手镯存放在哪个库存格或实例 ID。
- 修复“免费赠送后手镯没有被取走、后宫资格没有写入，最终进入单人恋爱线”的问题；赠送后仍会尽量清理实物。
- 旧存档首次载入时扫描一次：已买入手镯的按实际持有数量恢复；尚未进入普通恋爱／告白、且同一只手镯仍在的失败赠送存档，会修复为已赠送。
- 已进入普通恋爱或已结婚的旧存档不会自动改线；请使用路线重置或新档。
- 兼容其他 MOD 移动、改名或改动手镯状态；未经本 MOD 买卖事件直接删除手镯不会自动扣减。
- 未改正文、路线结构、结局编号或排期；1.11 立绘选择与更早修复保留。

1.12: Harem bracelet possession fix

- Bracelet eligibility now uses buy, sell, buyback and gift event counts instead of an inventory-slot or instance-ID lookup.
- Fixed the case where giving the bracelet for free left it in storage and failed to register harem eligibility, causing the solo romance route.
- Existing saves scan once on first load: bought bracelets are counted from current possession; a failed gift is repaired only when the same bracelet is still present and the solo romance/confession has not started.
- Saves already in the solo romance or married are not retroactively changed; use the route reset or a new save.
- Mods that move, rename or retag the bracelet are tolerated. Deletions that bypass this mod's trade events are not automatically deducted.
- No story text, route structure, ending numbers or schedules changed. The 1.11 portrait selector and earlier fixes remain.
