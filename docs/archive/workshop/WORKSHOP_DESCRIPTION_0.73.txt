[h2]上层区的富家千金爱上在下层区开当铺的我：埃莉诺剧情扩展[/h2]

“呵，你们下层区管这种地方叫‘商店’啊？”

她是上层区最大水培农场前负责人的独女。母亲被以挪用公款罪关押，家族一夜崩塌，她跌进自己曾最看不起的下层区。

你，是这里的一家当铺老板。

靠识货、开价、留证和承担风险，先成为她愿意信任的人。你们可以成为平等的恋人、继续做朋友，也可以选择一份双方说清条件的侍从约定。她的骄傲、判断与救母决心始终保留。

[h2]English[/h2]
An expanded Eleanor story with equal romance, friendship and an optional attendant relationship, four story-gated Wilde projects, five Upper Level return visits and an independent case review. Includes original-style and Maiden's Pawnshop-style maid portraits; no other mod is required.

Version 0.73 brings business back into later visits: native appraisal work, an optional supply order, small stock lots, a real prepaid sample and restocking purchases. Three repeatable business visits rotate at low frequency while the current Wilde project is genuinely unaffordable, yielding priority to story and urgent events. Earnings require actual goods or services. Declining a purchase or missing the optional order does not end the relationship or undo the rescue.

The mother's locket now uses a compact, filled 2×2 footprint with matching display scale. Legacy items are migrated only into valid space; crowded items remain intact until moved. Chinese and English include 2107 keys each. All 34 offline regression groups and the native mod scan pass; gameplay balance awaits further in-game feedback.

Normal start on day 23; optional early-start perk on day 12. All-ages. Back up saves before updating; downgrading is not guaranteed.
